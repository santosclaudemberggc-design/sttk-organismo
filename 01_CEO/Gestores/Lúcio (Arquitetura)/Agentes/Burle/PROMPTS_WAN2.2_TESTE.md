## Grupo 1: Tópicos Fixos de Arquitetura (5 prompts)

### Prompt 1: Fachada Frontal com Volumetria Clara
**Tipo:** Render estático com pan leve
**Descrição curta:** Modern residential building facade with clean lines, light concrete and glass, afternoon light from the east, shadow play on volumes
**Versão longa (para WAN):**
```
A contemporary residential building facade photographed from street level in afternoon light. 
The facade features clean geometric volumes with light concrete surfaces and large glass panels. 
Shadows cast by architectural elements create visual depth. Sky is clear with warm golden light 
hitting the eastern face. Camera pans slowly upward to reveal 6 stories.
Duration: 10 seconds.
```

### Prompt 2: Piscina e Terraço (Lazer)
**Tipo:** Render dinâmico com movimento de câmera
**Descrição curta:** Rooftop terrace pool in tropical residential complex, morning light, palm trees, water reflections
**Versão longa (para WAN):**
```
A rooftop terrace pool in a luxury residential building overlooking a tropical city. 
The pool features clean water reflecting morning sunlight, with integrated lounge areas and tall palm trees. 
Wooden decking surrounds the pool. Camera slowly orbits the pool, revealing city skyline in the background 
and the glass enclosure of the building's upper floor. Natural light creates shimmering water reflections. 
Duration: 12 seconds.
```

### Prompt 3: Interior — Pátio Central
**Tipo:** Render imersivo com luz natural controlada
**Descrição curta:** Internal courtyard of office building, double height space, natural light from skylights, lush vegetation, people walking
**Versão longa (para WAN):**
```
An interior double-height courtyard in a modern office building. Natural light pours from large skylights above, 
illuminating a central garden with native plants and small trees. Polished concrete floors reflect sunlight. 
People in casual dress move through the space, working at informal seating areas. Wood and glass materials 
define the architecture. Camera moves through the space at human walking pace, revealing different vantage points. 
Duration: 15 seconds.
```

### Prompt 4: Detalhe — Entrada Principal
**Tipo:** Render close-up com foco em materialidade
**Descrição curta:** Building entrance with dramatic overhang, wooden doors, stone detailing, evening light creating silhouette
**Versão longa (para WAN):**
```
A close-up view of a building entrance featuring a dramatic concrete overhang that casts deep shadows. 
Large wooden pivot doors with horizontal grain stand beneath. Stone cladding in warm earth tones frames the entry. 
Evening golden light creates a strong silhouette effect. The camera slowly zooms in on the door detail, 
then pulls back to reveal the full entry composition. Subtle wind rustles the nearby landscaping. 
Duration: 8 seconds.
```

### Prompt 5: Austeridade e Minimalismo
**Tipo:** Render cinematic com atmosfera clara
**Descrição curta:** Minimalist residential block, gray concrete, large windows, no ornamentation, clean lines, cool north light
**Versão longa (para WAN):**
```
A minimalist residential building in austere gray concrete with no decorative elements. 
Large horizontal window bands break the facade into strict geometric sections. The building is photographed 
in cool, diffused north-facing light. Surroundings are minimal landscaping with low grasses. 
The camera approaches the building slowly, revealing the exact proportions and material textures of the concrete. 
No people, no movement, pure geometry. Duration: 10 seconds.
```

## Grupo 2: Acervo + Inventados (5 prompts)

### Prompt 6: Estacionamento Subterrâneo (Inventado)
**Tipo:** Render técnico com circulação
**Descrição curta:** Underground parking garage, fluorescent strip lighting, clean lines, circulation ramp visible
**Versão longa (para WAN):**
```
An underground parking garage in a modern residential complex. Fluorescent lighting strips run along the ceiling, 
creating even illumination across the gray polished concrete floor. White painted columns support the structure in a regular grid. 
A gentle circulation ramp curves away in the background. Several parked vehicles are visible in designated spaces. 
The camera follows the ramp at slow speed, revealing the linear perspective of the structure. 
Clean, functional, well-lit environment. Duration: 10 seconds.
```

### Prompt 7: Fachada Noturna com Iluminação Arquitetônica
**Tipo:** Render dramático com efeitos de luz
**Descrição curta:** Building facade at night, architectural uplighting in warm color temperature, dark sky, reflections in glass
**Versão longa (para WAN):**
```
A residential building photographed at night with professional architectural uplighting in warm amber tones. 
The lights graze the concrete surfaces, emphasizing texture and volume. Large glass panels reflect the city lights beyond. 
The night sky is deep blue with barely visible stars. The camera slowly pans across the lit facade, 
then pulls back to show the building's relationship to surrounding urban lights in the distance. 
Duration: 12 seconds.
```

### Prompt 8: Escada Hélicoidal (Detalhe Acústico)
**Tipo:** Render de movimento vertical
**Descrição curta:** Spiral staircase in open lobby, natural light from above, cast shadows of treads on walls, quiet afternoon
**Versão longa (para WAN):**
```
A sculptural spiral staircase in a tall open lobby, photographed in natural daylight from a skylight above. 
The concrete treads cast sharp geometric shadows on the enclosing walls, creating a pattern of light and shadow. 
Each turn of the spiral reveals new proportions and material details. The camera follows the spiral upward in a continuous movement, 
creating a sense of ascent. Minimalist aesthetic, no people visible. Duration: 12 seconds.
```

### Prompt 9: Simulação de Uso — Comum Residencial Aberto
**Tipo:** Render com atividade humana
**Descrição curta:** Shared residential common area, families using furniture, natural light, warm color palette, afternoon activity
**Versão longa (para WAN):**
```
A shared residential common area in a mid-rise building, furnished with modern modular seating in warm earth tones. 
Large windows provide natural afternoon light. Several families and individuals are present using the space—sitting, reading, 
conversing in small groups. Wooden cladding on accent walls, polished concrete floors. Plants in large containers add greenery. 
The camera slowly moves through the space, passing groups of people, revealing comfortable scale and utility. 
Casual, inviting atmosphere. Duration: 15 seconds.
```

### Prompt 10: Transição Exterior-Interior (Varanda)
**Tipo:** Render imersivo com mudança de ambiência
**Descrição curta:** Transition from building interior to balcony overlooking city, morning light, indoor plants, urban view beyond
**Versão longa (para WAN):**
```
A interior living space transitions to a generous balcony overlooking a city. Inside, warm morning light enters through floor-to-ceiling 
glass doors. Indoor potted plants sit beside the glass. The camera moves through the glass door onto the balcony, revealing an unobstructed 
urban view in the distance—buildings, streets, movement. The balcony has a low glass safety rail and wooden flooring that matches the interior. 
The transition emphasizes the permeability between inside and outside. Duration: 12 seconds.
```
